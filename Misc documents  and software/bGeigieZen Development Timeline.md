### 🧭 Summit Highlights:

- **Simplified build**: Reduced from \~60 parts to \~18; assembly time cut to \~20–60 minutes; no soldering required for most components ([bgeigiezen.safecast.jp][5]).
- **Better hardware**: M5Stack Core processor, improved 320×240 color display, upgraded GPS (Beitian module, evolving to BZ-251/M9), larger onboard storage, and expandable I²C support ([bgeigiezen.safecast.jp][7]).
- **Enhanced user experience**: Audio click feedback, OTA updates, BLE/Wi-Fi connectivity, USB‑C port—all designed for accessibility and robustness ([Safecast][3], [safecast.org][6]).
